## Summary

The experiment shows that RAG provides a **modest improvement over the baseline**, particularly for closed-question accuracy. However, the statistical tests do not provide sufficient evidence to claim a significant improvement.

The top-k sweep also suggests that **more retrieved context is not necessarily better** for this dataset. In this experiment, retrieving a single highly relevant item produced the strongest closed-question performance.

Overall, the findings provide a useful baseline for investigating retrieval quality, context selection, and the relationship between retrieved evidence and downstream VQA performance.
